How does an Art Broker work?

Your wallet keeps the Gogh Punk and controls every owner action.

The Punk Wallet is the Punk’s separate on-chain wallet for collected NFTs.

The hosted agent scouts only within the limits you authorize. Autonomous execution remains bounded: paid mints, selling, arbitrary calls, and main-wallet spending remain blocked.

Rarity scheduling gives rarer Punks a small, bounded head start inside the owner-fair hosted rotation. It never skips another owner’s turn, guarantees a mint, or bypasses ownership, authorization, price, runtime, policy, or simulation checks.
