How control works

The Punk holder controls this wallet.

Your Gogh Punk stays in your main wallet. Its separate Punk Wallet holds collected assets. A transfer of the controlling Punk pauses paid authority until the new holder explicitly reauthorizes it.

Safety boundary

  • Main-wallet funds are never an autonomous spending source.
  • Autonomous selling, arbitrary calldata, and general token approvals remain blocked.
  • Live ownership is rechecked before every privileged action.
