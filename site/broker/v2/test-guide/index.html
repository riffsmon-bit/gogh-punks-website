2. One confirmation → directed paid mint

Selected owner test · #93 · Peppies World

Mint one NFT from Peppies World for up to 0.0001 ETH.
  1. Send this in Talk. The Directed paid mint panel should open a fresh quote for exactly one NFT.
  2. Verify Peppies World: 0xb73f1d1aee57410d537d87b656e98b9d3df5b213. Check the mint price, fixed worker fee, total escrow, wallet network fee and expiry. The full quoted worker fee is charged only after successful delivery.
  3. Select Confirm mint budget in wallet and approve once. First use creates #93’s mint vault, so its network gas is higher than later authorizations.
  4. The worker checks every minute. Use Recheck paid mint until delivery is verified. A second purchase signature should not be requested.
  5. Verify the receipt and NFT token ID. The destination is #93’s Agent wallet, 0xcadcfd37e715bc031cf0cec7fa2335091c878c83. Check Collection and Activity too; current NFT ownership is verified when Collection loads.

If a review expires before any wallet request, cancel the unsent review and prepare a fresh quote. If a wallet request was made, recover its original transaction hash from wallet activity. Refreshing must not send it again.

If the mint stops or expires, review Mission cancellation, confirm it, then review Refund and withdraw. These are two separate wallet transactions. The refund contains unused mint escrow; network gas already spent is excluded.

3. Burn #1753 → teach #93 Rarity Eye

This permanently destroys #1753 and can remove access to its wallets, including assets received later. Its assets do not move to #93.

  1. Open Forge with #93 selected. Select Recheck selected test. Verify source #1753 and recipient #93.
  2. If shown, review and confirm Enable Forge. This administrator transaction unpauses the shared contracts for all owners while enabling only Rarity Eye research. Recheck its receipt.
  3. Review #1753 approval, read the fresh wallet and obligation checks, and confirm the token-specific approval. Review any nonstandard assets and off-chain obligations before proceeding.
  4. Review Burn #1753 → credit #93. Check the owner acknowledgement, type BURN 1753, and confirm in the wallet.
  5. Verify the burn receipt and exactly one new training credit on #93. Then use Recheck training.
  6. Claim initial rarity slots if offered, learn Rarity Eye, and equip it. These are separate transactions. Training waits for chain finality between steps; recent observations were about 14 minutes. Recheck saved receipts while settlement is pending.
  7. Compare #93, #94 and #95. The result should identify that three-token sample and SAMPLE_ONLY. Unequip Rarity Eye and verify its equipped research action becomes unavailable; re-equip and repeat.

Review the mode, gas cap, reserve, expiry and any collection restrictions. Confirm the required Agent setup. The worker should report checks in Activity; it should count a mint only after verifying its receipt. No eligible match is a valid outcome.

For a directed free mint, choose the exact-contract example and replace its placeholder with an open, supported collection. A paid collection must not silently become a free-mint mission.

5. Gas, ETH, WETH and NFTs

  • Agent gas: send “Fund gas.” Review the source, destination and a small amount you choose. Verify the balance change after the receipt.
  • ETH: open Fund. Review a small deposit or withdrawal and its exact destination before signing.
  • WETH: use Fund → ETH ↔ WETH to wrap and unwrap a small chosen amount. Verify both balances. Wrapping does not place a bid.
  • NFTs: open Collection and check custody. Use an enabled withdrawal control for an NFT held in the Punk Wallet. Agent-wallet custody is displayed separately.

6. Research and playbook skills

Use the examples for checking a collection link, teaching a scouting routine, and opening Forge’s research lab. Contract Detective, Rarity Eye and Market Scout should identify their sources or sample limits. A playbook skill does not consume a permanent training credit or authorize purchases.

Equipped Rarity Eye research is available after the burn, learning and equipment sequence above. The research lab is a separate read-only test surface.
