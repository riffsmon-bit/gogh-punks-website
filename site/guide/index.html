GOGH PUNKS · HOLDER GUIDE

YOUR PUNK.
YOUR RULES.

Start by exploring. You do not need to fund a Punk to see its collection or explore its controls.

1. Connect and choose

  1. Open Gogh Punks and choose Connect wallet.
  2. Use the wallet that owns your Gogh Punks on Robinhood Chain.
  3. Pick a Punk from the roster. Its number stays visible as you change tabs.

Signing in proves which wallet is yours. It is separate from approving a payment or asset transfer.

2. Choose your Punk’s rules

Open Actions. Choose a free-mint mission, add an art preference, and set the reserve, maximum network fee and collection limits. Select Review my rules, then inspect the full confirmation before saving.

Ask recommends. Assist prepares supported actions for approval. Autonomous actions need separate, bounded permission and a readiness check. These controls do not make AI calls.

Create wallet → fund → set rules → start mission

  1. Select a Punk. Its setup strip tells you what is verified; Check setup means it has not been checked recently.
  2. Open Fund. If its Agent wallet does not exist, review and confirm wallet creation only. This creates the wallet; it does not grant mission permission or start hunting. Skip creation if the wallet already exists.
  3. Choose Add Agent gas. ETH in the ordinary Punk Wallet is separate from the Agent’s gas. Choose the funding source and amount, review the simulation, then confirm the exact deposit in your wallet. Keep enough ETH above the protected reserve for minting gas.
  4. Open Actions, choose your free-mint rules and select Review my rules. Check the limits, reserve, target and expiry.
  5. Choose Start mission when you are ready. Review and confirm the separate, bounded minting permission in your wallet. Creating the wallet and funding it do not automatically request this permission.
  6. Check status. Looking for mints requires a recent successful worker check. A pending result, depleted gas, protected reserve or paused worker is shown explicitly.

Your connected wallet pays the network fees for creation, funding and permission changes; keep some ETH there. Funding never grants a new mission permission. Each mint still has to pass your limits and safety checks. Keep hunting lasts up to 100 mints or 30 days; review a renewal when either limit is reached.

You do not need to Recall Punk to add gas to an existing Agent wallet. If its permission is still active, adding gas can let it resume collecting within the approved rules. Recall first only if you want to stop that permission; do not authorize an active mission again just to fund it.

Swarm · multiple Punks

  1. Before starting new missions, open each Punk’s Fund screen and create its Agent wallet if needed. Each creation requires a separate wallet confirmation and grants no minting permission.
  2. Fund the Agent wallets individually or through Swarm Wallet, described below. The existing optional Fund Swarm planner also splits one total budget into per-Punk deposits; each of those deposits needs its own wallet confirmation. Network fees are additional.
  3. Open Actions → Swarm · multiple Punks. Select up to 10 Punks owned by your connected wallet. Choose a free-mint search, or enter one Robinhood Chain collection contract for directed free mints.
  4. Set daily and total limits per Punk, or choose Keep hunting for up to 100 mints over 30 days. Two Punks at five each can collect up to ten in total. Each keeps its own funds, reserve, gas cap, taste and safety rules.
  5. Choose Review Swarm Plan, then Review Punk to see its selected limits without opening MetaMask. Choose Sign in & load full rules when ready. If needed, MetaMask asks for a free login signature.
  6. Read the complete rules, then choose Start mission and confirm permission for that Punk. Review and authorize the other Punks separately. Shared discovery operates without leaving this page open.

Planning opens no wallet request. For the individual funding planner, finish one deposit before opening the next. A prepared funding transaction cannot change within that batch. If another wallet transaction makes an unsent review unusable, check all original deposits in wallet activity, close the planner, then create a fresh budget for only the Punks that still need gas. Never repeat a pending deposit. After a pending or missing wallet result, use the original transaction recovery before funding again. Only a confirmed matching deposit is marked funded.

Closing the planner does not pause authorized missions; use Recall Punk on each Punk if you want to stop its permission. Recall is not required to add gas; funding an active Punk may resume its existing mission. After a lost wallet response or browser restart, check the original Punk’s status before retrying. Swarm supports eligible free mints only. Paid mints remain separate, individually reviewed actions.

Swarm Wallet · your shared gas budget

  1. Open Swarm Wallet above the selected Punk controls, then choose Check Swarm Wallet. If needed, review and confirm creation of your owner-controlled wallet.
  2. Review and deposit the ETH budget you want to keep there. Your connected wallet pays the transaction fee.
  3. Create each intended recipient’s Agent wallet through its Fund screen first. Select 1–10 currently owned Punks and enter a total ETH amount.
  4. Choose Review gas batch. The review lists Punks in number order and splits the amount equally, assigning any smallest-unit remainder to lower Punk numbers. Check each destination, amount, total and network fee. The maximum is 1 ETH per Punk and 10 ETH per batch.
  5. Confirm this batch in your wallet. Its transfers succeed together or none do. Each future batch needs another confirmation; there are no automatic refills.
  6. After funding, set and review mission rules in Actions. Choose Start mission and confirm the permission for each Punk separately.
  7. To return unused ETH, choose Review withdrawal and confirm. Only the Swarm Wallet owner can withdraw, only to that owner’s wallet.

Keep ETH in your connected wallet for the network fees on creation, deposits, batches and withdrawals. Allocated ETH stays in each Punk’s Agent Account and follows that Punk’s ownership if transferred. Withdrawing from the Swarm Wallet returns only unallocated ETH still there; it does not pull funds back from Punks.

Funding grants no new minting permission. An already-active Punk may resume collecting when funded; it does not need Recall just to receive gas. If a transaction result is unknown, use Check transaction with the original, speed-up or cancellation hash instead of submitting again. The individual funding planner remains available and does not use the Swarm Wallet balance.

3. Check a link

In Actions, choose Check a link and paste the project or contract link. Read the chain, contract, price and any missing information. A supported contract check may identify a mint; it does not guarantee that the project is safe or that a purchase is available.

Checking a link does not spend funds. If a link is unsupported, use the exact contract address or keep the result for review.

4. Understand the wallets

Fund shows the selected Punk's balances, reserve and available budget. The Punk Wallet and Agent Account are separate addresses. Each review names the destination; check it before approving.

Funding costs the amount you choose plus a network fee. The funds belong to that Punk's account, controlled by its current owner. Buying or selling the Punk changes who controls it.

5. View and withdraw assets

Collection shows discovered NFTs after checking custody. Choose an NFT's withdrawal action to review its account and recipient.

For tokens, use the token withdrawal control when available. Choose the account that holds them, enter the exact token contract, and inspect its balance before entering an amount. The review sends supported tokens to your connected owner wallet. Unsupported token behavior blocks the action.

Older wallet versions have their own recovery links. A failed inventory request means the balance is unknown, not empty. If a wallet request loses its result, recover the original transaction instead of submitting again.

6. Review a paid mint

  1. Open Actions → Direct a paid mint.
  2. Copy the NFT collection contract address from its Robinhood Chain explorer page. Paste it into NFT collection contract, then select Check mint contract.
  3. The mint must be open, use the reviewed SeaDrop Studio contract code and cost at most 0.001 ETH for one NFT. Other mint websites and contract types are unsupported.
  4. If its Agent wallet does not exist, open Fund and review wallet creation only. Creation has its own network fee and does not start a mission.
  5. Select Review & mint now, check the exact price, destination and maximum network fee, and confirm in your wallet.
  6. Recheck the original mint until delivery is confirmed. The NFT goes to the Agent Account, not your owner wallet. Use its original transaction hash for recovery if needed.

Review & mint now is a direct, owner-confirmed mint. Your wallet pays the exact mint price, and the NFT goes to the selected Punk's Agent Account. It does not start an unattended shopping mission. A missing Agent wallet must be created first; a paid mint does not need an autonomous mission permission.

A funded older mission is not proof of delivery. Its original status, receipt, cancellation and recovery controls remain separate. Do not fund a replacement because a result is slow.

7. Train and equip

Open Forge to check learned skills, slots and available training routes. Learned means your Punk keeps the skill; equipped means it is active in a slot.

Burning permanently destroys the sacrificed Punk. Its assets do not move to the trained Punk. Unknown assets, attached funds or unresolved work must block the review. General holder burning remains unavailable until its release checks are complete; previous burn receipts can be checked in Settings by the eligible owner; that recovery view cannot start a new burn.

The optional paid-credit route costs 0.0005 ETH per credit, plus the network fee and starts with an approved owner test. Select a Punk, open Forge, then choose Recheck paid training if available. Review a credit purchase and confirm it in your wallet. A review lasts at most one minute; use Refresh unsent review if it expires, check the refreshed fee, then confirm. If a wallet request was already made, recover its original transaction instead. After its receipt is confirmed, close the saved review and recheck. Review paid training setup once, then learn Rarity Eye and equip it; each step needs its own wallet confirmation. Purchased credits stay with that Punk and cannot be refunded. Extra slots require its starting slot allocation to be claimed first. Payments stay blocked while purchases are paused. Other holders cannot buy through the website yet. Only released skills with working tools can be learned; a catalog card alone does not make a skill available.

8. Stay in control

Select your Punk and press Recall Punk beside its mission status, including when it says Reserve reached. Confirm the recall in MetaMask and wait for the confirmed result. The connected wallet pays the network fee. Recall revokes the mission permission and pauses its strategy; funds stay in the Punk’s wallets. Use Withdraw separately if you want to recover assets. Actions → Pause opens the same recall flow. A transaction already submitted still needs its original receipt checked. Transferring a Punk does not carry the previous owner's spending authorization to the new holder.

Public floor sweeps, WETH bids and broad autonomous paid spending remain disabled. Wrapping ETH into WETH does not place a bid.

If something fails, keep the displayed message and any public transaction hash. Never share a private key or recovery phrase.

Your saved strategy

Strategy shows your confirmed preferences, spending limits, reserve and safety rules. Change them in Actions and review the complete draft. A saved strategy does not itself grant wallet permission. To start collecting automatically, first create and fund its Agent wallet through Fund. Then choose START FREE-MINT MISSION beside your selected Punk. It carries over your saved limits and shows the complete free-mint rules; Start mission requests the separate wallet permission after your review. If a completed mission left an old collection target, choose New free-mint search · Clear target. Your other saved rules stay in place; review and authorize the new mission. ASK only recommends; ASSIST requires your approval for each mint. Check the status strip to confirm whether the worker is looking, waiting or needs attention.

Keep hunting

In Actions, choose Keep hunting · up to 100 mints / 30 days, then review and authorize the mission. Your Punk keeps looking within your daily limit while its gas, reserve and safety rules allow. You can pause it sooner. The current wallet permission stops at 100 mints or 30 days and must be renewed in your wallet; this is not unlimited spending or an indefinite permission.

Follow progress in Activity

Check mission status, completed mints and the last check. Each event explains a result or rejection. An active mission or funded budget does not prove NFT delivery: check the confirmed transaction and Collection. Viewing activity sends no transaction. Roster and Activity badges show unread completed, failed, expired or attention-needed missions. Open Mission updates or Activity to review them. These updates are saved in your current browser and use existing status checks; they are not phone or email alerts.
