The manifesto

Fine art was never supposed to behave.

Gogh Punks pulls post-impressionist motion out of the museum and into the underground. Every portrait carries its own character, but the collection shares one visual language: heavy pixels, electric skies, strange beauty, and a refusal to color inside the lines.

Built for collectors, creators, punks, and anyone who believes digital art deserves a little more soul—and a little less permission.
